How Data & Updates Work

1. Where Student Data Is Saved

All student profiles, event details, registrations, QR ticket IDs, and attendance logs are persisted in a relational SQLite database (hypeup.db) with foreign-key integrity and WAL mode.

2. Who Updates Events

Faculty Coordinators propose and manage department events. Admins review, approve/reject, and publish events. Every change is recorded in an Audit Log.

3. 3-Role Access Control

Separate authenticated portals for Students, Faculty, and Admins using scrypt password hashing and role-verified tokens.
